I build generative AI models that predict how cells respond to genetic and chemical perturbations, helping prioritise experiments *in silico* before they are run in the lab. My broader goal is to predict which perturbations could shift cells towards a desired state or reverse a disease-associated one, to identify candidate therapeutic targets for drug discovery.

During my PhD at the Wellcome Sanger Institute and the University of Cambridge, I co-led the development of **PerturbGen**, a generative model that predicts how perturbations reshape cell differentiation over time. I developed core model architectures and led an *in silico* screen in human blood stem and progenitor cells, which nominated regulators that collaborators subsequently validated experimentally. I am now developing models that connect gene expression with cell morphology from high-throughput perturbation screens, linking molecular responses to changes in cellular phenotype.

I bring hands-on experience in experimental biology and software development for pharma R&D. At ETH Zürich, I engineered CRISPR-edited stem cell lines, grew cerebral organoids and carried out perturbation screens myself. At Roche Pharma R&D in Basel, I built end-to-end data pipelines using cloud computing and worked in Agile/Scrum teams to develop software for lab scientists. Having generated experimental data and built tools for the scientists who use it, I care about making model predictions testable and computational methods useful in day-to-day research.

I am looking for machine learning scientist or data scientist roles where I can bring these skills to drug discovery, working with experimental teams to prioritise experiments and investigate candidate therapeutic targets. I am interested in pharma, biotech start-ups and technology companies working in health and the life sciences, ideally in Switzerland, London or Cambridge, starting in early 2027. Feel free to get in touch via [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kevin-chi-hao-ly).

Outside work, you'll usually find me hiking in the mountains or on the climbing wall.
